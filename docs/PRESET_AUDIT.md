# Dated competitive preset audit

Reviewed October 26, 2026 against fresh official articles. This checks list composition and card availability; it does not certify matchup strategy, AI strength, sideboarding, or full Standard conformance. The [machine-readable audit](../assets/data/preset_audits/2026-10-26/deck-audit.json) pins the catalog, preset manifest, and original HTML hashes. Compressed source pages are archived beside it.

All four presets match the published 40-card composition, chosen champion, legend identity, rune split, and recorded battlefield names. The three Los Angeles lists come from Riot's [September 30 article](https://playriftbound.com/en-us/news/organizedplay/los-angeles-top-decks/); the historical Kai'Sa list comes from Riot's [June 4 Vancouver article](https://playriftbound.com/en-us/news/organizedplay/vancouvers-top-decks/). Both events predate Radiance, so these remain dated competitive examples.

The client currently selects one supported battlefield for a single game. It does not yet model a tournament series, three-field registration/selection, or sideboarding. The Arena's Greatest remains historical provenance only and is not playable. A fully supported sideboard card pool alone does not establish sideboarding support.

The Los Angeles article contains 41 deck sections representing 36 distinct pilot/result lists; all five repeated lists agree exactly. Its winning Rengar main deck needs four additional identities: Irresistible Faefolk, Kinkou Initiate, Nidalee, Cat Form, and Pyke, Dockside Butcher. Its legend and chosen champion are already available. The same six legal battlefield additions listed above would complete the three Los Angeles presets' field options and the winner's field pool. Pyke's alternate printing and current battlefield errata require their own source and runtime review before admission.

Name matching uses catalog identities and exact normalized names. Legend names also use the printed champion tag and title. The sole article spelling alias is “Trapping Ground” to the gallery's “Trapping Grounds,” recorded explicitly in the audit. Master Yi, Wuju Bladesman maps to `ogs-019-024`; it is legal in Standard and banned in 2v2. It is distinct from Master Yi, Wuju Master (`unl-191-219`). No approximate name matching or substitution of a similar card is used.
