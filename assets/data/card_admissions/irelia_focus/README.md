# Decree of Focus

Reviewed November 11, 2026 from the accepted official gallery archive and the
complete original official card face. The single VEN-040/166 printing agrees
with the gallery: Calm, Spell, 1 Energy, no Power, Reaction. There is no listed
erratum or unresolved printing conflict. The admission records the original
image, bounded client image, raw text and unchanged predecessor catalog hashes.

The target must be friendly and currently either in combat with an enemy Fury
unit or chosen by an enemy Fury spell on the chain. Abilities, unchosen area
damage, and a token's factory card do not satisfy the spell/domain condition.
Repeat targets count. The restriction is checked when choosing and again on
resolution, following Core 355 and 359.3.e. The ordinary temporary-Might effect
then gives the legal target +4 this turn. Choosing Irelia still creates her normal
choose triggers.

This card appears in both published Los Angeles Irelia sideboards. It is available
for custom practice decks; this admission does not claim match sideboarding.
