# Orianna, Exquisite — accepted admission

Accepted November 11, 2026. Both complete official faces
(RAD-042/167 and RAD-042a/167) were individually inspected on November 11, 2026.
They agree on the card's stats, tags, Shield, and friendly-gear-here restriction;
only the Shield reminder is omitted on the alternate face. The complete identity
review preserves both printed strings and every predecessor review.

The admission registers runtime ID430. It retains 4 Might without gear, gains the
normal +1 Might while defending, and deals zero combat damage unless friendly
gear is at its actual location. Attached gear follows its bearer, including a
different friendly unit. Unattached gear and Gold count; readiness and inactive
printed gear abilities do not remove the gear's presence. Enemy gear, facedown
cards, and gear elsewhere do not satisfy the condition. Stun still prevents
combat damage when the gear requirement is met. Other effects that reference
Might, including Fight, retain its full value.

The API reports the current requirement and whether it is met alongside combat
contribution. Native previews explain why combat damage is zero or enabled.
Beginner and Advanced Mote tips explain the nearby gear interaction.

The newer Irelia lead is Arito's list attributed to second place at Convergence IV
on October 31, 2026: three Orianna and one Gust Monk. The organizer's event page
confirms the event and Radiance pool; its JavaScript standings have not yet been
independently captured here. This addition is not a new verified preset.
Existing tournament recipes remain unchanged, and test games use a separate
explicit 40-card Irelia variant.
