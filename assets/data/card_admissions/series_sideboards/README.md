# Los Angeles sideboard spells

Decree of Strength (433, VEN-085/166) and Lacerate (434, VEN-127/166)
complete the saved Rengar and Kennen sideboards, respectively. The original
744×1039 faces were inspected alongside the accepted official gallery snapshot.
The receipt preserves original and effective printing facts, source hashes,
original and runtime images, and the unchanged preceding 433 cards and six
main-deck recipes. Neither printing has a reviewed erratum or unresolved fact.

Decree reveals the other player's whole hand, accepts exactly one Mind card
when one exists, and recycles that card to its owner's deck bottom. Empty or
ineligible hands still reveal and have a Continue action. Repeat inspects the
remaining hand again. The API exposes the hand only during the reveal; scouting
may retain that legally learned information.

Lacerate chooses any unit. Removing Empowered precedes the live Might check;
the 3-Might threshold is a resolution condition, not a target restriction.
Buff responses, Deflect, counters, Irelia's choose triggers, physical target
identity, Empowered Deathknell and Guardian Angel use the common engine.
Printed Flow is 4 Energy plus 2 Order Power, with normal spell timing and the
existing delayed banish replacement, including on a countered spell.
