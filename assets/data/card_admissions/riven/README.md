# Riven pool source admission

This record covers An Offer You Can’t Refuse, Blitzcrank (Impassive), Bedeviling Stranger, Blade of the Exile, Riven (Broken Blade), The Exile and Baron Nashor, plus the auxiliary Baron Pit token. All twelve original official faces were individually inspected on November 12, 2026, including their collector footers, types, costs, tags and complete rules/equipment panels.

`admissions.json` preserves the complete archived printing projections, separate reviewed effective facts, original image hashes, manually observed face facts, variant membership and runtime mappings. `tools/test_riven_admissions.py` independently parses the archived gallery and checks these records. Source review alone does not enable engine support: removing the new runtime mappings leaves all eight identities unavailable.

`predecessor/` contains the exact pre-Riven evidence files from public commit 2a95ef6. The historical tests retain their original hashes and also verify that every old record and authority remains unchanged in the live manifests. New source reviews and the legend are appended.

The exact Sonecon Stuttgart registration is recorded separately in `assets/data/preset_admissions/riven_stuttgart_2026_11/`. The printed-card audit corrected the draft lesson: Blade grants +3 Might and Ganking, and Riven’s three modes are Buff, spend Buff to Stun, and spend Buff to ready. Neither card has the Energy generation or extra-play effect that appeared in an earlier unpublished lesson.

The [release acceptance](../../../../docs/validation/riven_stuttgart.json) records the completed Rust, source, Nix, installed API and native UI checks. This admission does not establish full Standard coverage.
