# Reckless Innovator — accepted admission

Accepted November 11, 2026. Its original official face,
runtime image, effective facts, printing cohort, and unchanged predecessor cards
and presets are pinned in [admissions.json](admissions.json).

The gallery omits its printed Piltover tag. The independent
[source review](../../catalog_evidence/reviews/reckless-innovator.json) preserves
the raw empty list and corrects only the effective tag. The preceding field-review
ledger remains archived byte for byte, and its records remain exact prefixes of
the current ledger. A source correction alone does not admit a playable card.

The unit costs 3 Energy, has 3 Might, and may pay an additional 1 Energy and
1 Chaos Power to enter ready through Accelerate. Entering exhausted is not an
Exhaust transition. Each later Ready-to-Exhausted transition triggers a mandatory
choice: play one Gold token exhausted at Base, or pay 1 Chaos Power to draw one.
There is no once-per-turn restriction.

The November 12 audit corrects the original payment timing. Choose the mode
before responses (Core 355.3); the later draw cost is paid during resolution
(Core 383.3.b). Riot's [Radiance FAQ](https://playriftbound.com/en-us/news/organizedplay/radiance-rules-faq-and-clarifications/)
explains that distinction for Riven, Broken Blade. Applying the same instruction
structure to Innovator is an explicit inference from that ruling and the core
rule. The original acceptance receipt's pre-response assertion is superseded.

Declining the resolving cost skips only the draw (445.2.b), preserving completed
Add actions and never switching to Gold. Undo payment separately restores the
staged attempt. The finalized effect retains its mode after source departure.
The engine, native controls, bot API and Beginner/Advanced coaching share this
sequence. The original card/source admission is unchanged.
