# Battle Preparations — Irelia priority

The complete official RAD-032/167 face was visually inspected November 11, 2026.
It is a Calm Reaction spell costing 2 Energy and 0 Power. The archived identity has
one printing and no erratum or field correction. Original and bounded runtime art,
raw/effective facts, and the preceding 426-card/five-preset hashes are pinned in
`admissions.json`. Runtime ID 426 follows Hunker Down. This adds a practice-pool
card; it does not relabel a community list as an official tournament preset.

Core 832.1.c–d places the optional Show Off choice during finalization. The player
may reveal a gear in their own hand or pick a friendly board gear, including an
attached gear or Gold. This is a pick, not a target (355.7). The selected object's
information is captured before payment; spending shown Gold for the spell's cost
does not undo Show Off (832.1.c.4). The revealed card is public, but the rest of the
hand and its private ordering stay hidden. Declining Show Off is legal.

Choose one unit, then zero, one or two distinct physical runes if showing off.
The card does not restrict either choice to friendly objects. Already-ready runes
are legal, including runes that will be exhausted for the Energy cost. Recycling
a chosen rune during payment invalidates that target at the final legality check
(357.3, 358.1), allowing Undo or refusal. Recycling it after finalization makes
only that particular resolution instruction fail; a newly channelled rune of the
same domain does not replace it. Losing the unit does not cancel the independent
rune instructions (359.3.e.5). Countering the spell prevents both instructions.

Repeat repeats resolution instructions, not Show Off (820.1.d.2). Each execution
chooses its own unit and optional runes during finalization (820.2); all executions
use the one captured Show Off choice. Rune choices do not trigger Irelia's
unit-choice ability. Native, AI and API callers use the same typed play choices.
