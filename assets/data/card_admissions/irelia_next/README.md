# Find Your Center and Hush — Irelia priority

Both cards are implemented for the Standard Irelia card pool. Complete original
official faces were inspected November 11, 2026. Find Your Center has one gallery
printing, OGN-047/298, and agrees with the archived raw facts. Hush has one gallery
printing, RAD-041/167; its existing reviewed type/domain corrections remain
separate from the unchanged raw gallery record. The face confirms a Calm Spell,
1 Energy and 2 Calm Power. Neither card has a listed erratum.

Find Your Center draws one card, then channels one rune exhausted. It always draws
when it resolves, even with an empty Rune Deck; it does not draw an extra card
when channeling fails. At an opponent score of five or more in the eight-point
Standard game, its total Energy cost is reduced by two. Cost previews and staged
payments use the same condition. Draw-induced burnout victory stops further play.

Hush can counter physical spells whose printed or copied Power cost is at least
two, following Core 131.3, 204.1.a and 356.1.c. Additional costs, discounts and payments do
not change that characteristic. It never counters an activated/triggered ability.
The ordinary chain, exact payment and counter-resolution systems remain in use.

The runtime IDs are 422 and 423, following Decree of Focus. A secondary-source Stuttgart list
attributes two main-deck Find Your Center and one sideboard Hush to DonCapucino;
that list is a priority lead, not a newly published preset or a sideboarding claim.
