# Kha'Zix, Mutating Horror

Development admission for optional Chaos practice builds, including Irelia.
Runtime and installed acceptance are pending. The existing tournament recipes
remain the sourced lists; this admission does not create a new meta preset.

Both complete original official faces were individually inspected on November
14, 2026 UTC. They agree on Champion Unit, Kha'Zix / The Void, four Energy,
one Chaos Power, four Might, Ambush, and the attack/defend instructions. The
alternate face omits only the italic Ambush reminder. Original images and their
hashes are recorded in the printing review and `admissions.json`.

The [official Core Rules](https://cmsassets.rgpub.io/sanity/files/dsfx7636/news_live/5c7946f7c3b8c6e0faff85ab6dadc6d2e721ea94.pdf),
linked by the [Rules Hub](https://playriftbound.com/en-us/rules-hub/) on review,
support these distinctions:

- The enemy must be alone at Kha'Zix's battlefield; friendly company does not
  prevent the trigger (740.2.a).
- The condition belongs to the triggering event and is not checked again when
  the ability resolves (383.2.a.1).
- Attack and Defend conditions are checked at the unit's first respective
  designation in each combat (383.4.e.2 and 383.4.f.2).
- The Might lasts this turn. If Kha'Zix leaves after triggering, the independent
  XP instruction still does as much as possible (055).
- Ambush permits reaction timing at a battlefield containing a friendly unit;
  it does not grant reaction timing at base (822.1).

Controlled-position tests distinguish these cases from complete-game evidence.
The generated card inventory records coverage; admission metadata alone is not
proof of executable or fully verified rules support.
