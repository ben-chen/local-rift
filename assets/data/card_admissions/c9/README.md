Every candidate has one gallery printing, is Standard legal in the archived snapshot, has no indexed erratum, and has no unresolved gameplay or variant field after the applicable reviews. This is a bounded, dated admission record, not a promise that every unflagged source is correct or that the full Standard rules are implemented.
