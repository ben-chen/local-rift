# Arito’s Convergence IV Irelia preset

The original [organizer deck page](https://topdeck.gg/deck/convergence-4/3Mb0hLsUTwgBYrpAauGOaRvDhnq1)
and [standings](https://topdeck.gg/bracket/convergence-4) were read on November 11,
2026: Arito placed second with an 8–2–0 record in the 272-player October 31 event.
The rendered deck entries were reread individually; no downloaded deck export is
claimed. `admission.json` records the transcription and `preset.json` the exact
runtime recipe.

Preset 5 uses legend 0 (Irelia, Blade Dancer), with Irelia, Fervent as its chosen
champion. The displayed 39 main cards plus that chosen champion form the 40-card
main list. Its ten sideboard cards stay separate. The runes are six Calm and six
Chaos; battlefields are Abandoned Hall, Sunken Temple and Targon’s Peak. It
includes two Edge of Night, three Orianna and one Gust Monk. Earlier recipes
and all ten legend training lists remain unchanged.

The library’s three tabs expose the main deck, sideboard and battlefield rules.
Select this preset for either seat to choose that seat’s battlefield before play.
Sideboard cards can be inspected or practiced through a custom deck copy; this
addition does not implement between-game sideboarding or best-of-three matches.
The bot catalog exposes the same registration data.
